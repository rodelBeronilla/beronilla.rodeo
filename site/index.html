Frameworks and standards

ISO/IEC 27001:2022The international standard for an information security management system. Certification follows an accredited audit against the Annex A controls the organization declares applicable.
ISO/IEC 27002:2022The companion guidance to 27001: how to implement each control, organized into organizational, people, physical and technological themes.
ISO/IEC 27017Cloud-specific security controls layered on ISO 27002, with responsibilities split between cloud providers and their customers.
SOC 2 Type 2An independent auditor's report on how a service organization's controls operated over a period, against the AICPA Trust Services Criteria.
NIST SP 800-53The US federal catalogue of security and privacy controls, used well beyond government as a baseline to build and assess against.
NIST SP 800-171Requirements for protecting controlled unclassified information in non-federal systems, and the basis of CMMC for defence suppliers.
PHIPAOntario's Personal Health Information Protection Act: how health information custodians may collect, use and disclose personal health information.
PIPEDACanada's federal private-sector privacy law, the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act, built on ten fair information principles.
HIPAAThe US Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act; its Privacy and Security Rules set the baseline for protected health information.
