How I work

The detail is the job.

I work in security, risk and compliance, currently at TELUS Health, where I run internal ISO 27001 and SOC 2 assessments, lead readiness engagements, and review changes for their impact on our controls and obligations. What I enjoy most is the depth: reading a standard carefully, working out what evidence actually proves a control is operating, and getting the details right.

  • Evidence first

    I would rather get an answer right than get it quickly. When someone tells me a control is working, the interesting question is what would prove it, and I will go and find that before I write anything down.

  • Dig to the root

    I read a standard clause by clause rather than from someone's summary, and I want to understand how a system actually works before I trust what it reports about itself. The findings worth having usually sit one layer below where people stop looking.

  • Build the tooling that makes data usable

    Repetitive analysis by hand is a poor use of anyone. I build the thing around it instead: intake pipelines that validate and route records, dashboards and reporting that give a team a picture they can decide from. I have been doing this since an internship, where it was security awareness dashboards, through to the automation and reporting I maintain today.
